Responsible Gaming

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Real-money online skill gaming involves an element of financial risk and may be addictive. Yono Hub advocates for healthy gaming habits and expects players to participate responsibly.

1. Responsible Play Tips

  • Set a Budget: Only play with cash that you can afford to lose. Never use funds reserved for daily necessities, rent, or food.
  • Don't Chase Losses: If you lose a match, accept the outcome. Do not increase your deposit limits in an attempt to make up for losses.
  • Take Breaks: Monitor your gameplay time. Step away if you feel anxious, frustrated, or notice it interfering with work, relationships, or health.
  • Treat it as Entertainment: Gaming is designed to test skills and offer casual fun, not to serve as a primary source of regular income.

2. Restricted Play Rules (18+ Only)

Online real-money cash matches are strictly prohibited for minors under the age of 18. Age verification gates are active on our portal to enforce this regulatory standard.

3. Restricted Indian Territories

Under local state regulations, players residing in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Odisha, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tamil Nadu must not play online cash games. Real money platforms restrict access from these IP locations automatically, and players must comply with these state laws.

4. Finding Support & Help

If you or someone you know is showing signs of gaming addiction or compulsive playing behaviors, we encourage seeking professional advice or counseling. Many online support portals offer advice, lock-out options, and self-exclusion tools to limit gaming profile access.
