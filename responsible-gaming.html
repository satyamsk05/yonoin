Responsible Gaming

Last updated: August 8, 2026

At Yono Hub, we advocate for clean, skilled, and highly responsible gaming. Online skill-based and real-money gaming should serve exclusively as entertainment options and not as regular income pathways.

1. Financial Risks & Warnings

All featured applications involve real financial stakes. Cash gaming carries the inherent risk of financial losses and can become habit-forming or addictive. We urge all users to play responsibly and establish limits on time and capital before playing.

2. Age Restrictive Protocols (18+)

Our website and the apps listed are strictly intended for players 18 years of age and older. We advise parents and guardians to utilize filtering tools or monitoring software to prevent minors from accessing cash-gaming websites.

3. State Restrictions

Real-money online skill gaming is prohibited in several Indian states. Accessing these games is forbidden if you reside in: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Odisha, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka. Please comply with local rules before choosing to proceed.

4. Healthy Gaming Guidelines

  • Set Budgets: Never deposit cash that you cannot afford to lose.
  • Time Limits: Set alarms and monitor session times to avoid excessive gaming sessions.
  • Avoid Chasing Losses: Accept losses as part of entertainment rather than chasing them.
  • Play in Modulated Moods: Do not play games when stressed, tired, or under the influence.
